The magic system in the wizarding world is vast and complex, encompassing spells, wands, potions, and various forms of advanced magical practice. Magic is an innate ability — a witch or wizard is born with it, and it typically manifests in childhood through uncontrolled bursts of magical energy during moments of strong emotion. Those born to magical families who lack magical ability are known as Squibs, while magical children born to non-magical parents are called Muggle-borns.

Wands are the primary tool through which witches and wizards channel their magic. Each wand is unique, crafted from a specific wood (such as holly, elder, vine, or yew) with a magical core at its centre. The three supreme cores used by the wandmaker Ollivander are phoenix feather, dragon heartstring, and unicorn hair. The wand chooses the wizard — a principle that Ollivander considers fundamental. A wizard using a wand that has not chosen them will produce inferior magic. Wands can change allegiance if their owner is disarmed or defeated. The Elder Wand, one of the three Deathly Hallows, is said to be the most powerful wand in existence, crafted by Death himself according to legend, though more likely made by Antioch Peverell.

Spells are broadly categorised into several types. Charms add properties to an object or creature (Wingardium Leviosa for levitation, Accio for summoning, Lumos for light, Expecto Patronum for conjuring a Patronus). Transfiguration changes the form or appearance of an object and is considered some of the most complex magic taught at Hogwarts. Hexes and jinxes are minor dark spells used in duelling — the Bat-Bogey Hex was Ginny Weasley's speciality. Curses are the most powerful and malevolent category of dark magic.

The three Unforgivable Curses are the darkest spells known to wizard-kind, and their use on another human being carries a life sentence in Azkaban. The Imperius Curse (Imperio) places the victim under the complete control of the caster, though it can be resisted with sufficient willpower. The Cruciatus Curse (Crucio) inflicts excruciating pain and was used extensively by Death Eaters during both Wizarding Wars — prolonged exposure can cause permanent insanity, as happened to Frank and Alice Longbottom. The Killing Curse (Avada Kedavra) causes instant death and leaves no trace of injury on the body. It produces a flash of green light and cannot be blocked by any known magical shield. The only person known to have survived it is Harry Potter, who was protected by the sacrificial love of his mother Lily.

Potions are a branch of magic that involves brewing magical mixtures in cauldrons following precise recipes. Unlike spells, potions can theoretically be brewed by anyone who follows the instructions correctly, though magical ability greatly aids the process. Notable potions include Polyjuice Potion (allows the drinker to assume the physical appearance of another person for one hour; takes a month to brew and requires a piece of the target person), Veritaserum (a powerful truth serum — three drops and the drinker will spill their innermost secrets), Felix Felicis (liquid luck — makes the drinker extraordinarily fortunate for a period, but is toxic in large quantities and banned in competitions), Amortentia (the most powerful love potion in existence, producing a powerful infatuation rather than true love, with a scent unique to each person based on what attracts them), and Wolfsbane Potion (allows a werewolf to retain their mind during transformation, though it does not prevent the transformation itself).

Apparition is the magical ability to teleport from one location to another instantaneously. It requires concentration on the "Three Ds" — Destination, Determination, and Deliberation. Done improperly, a witch or wizard risks Splinching — leaving part of their body behind. Apparition requires a licence (obtainable at age seventeen) and is impossible within the Hogwarts grounds due to protective enchantments. Side-Along Apparition allows an unlicensed person to travel by holding onto a licensed Apparator.

Animagi are witches and wizards who can transform at will into an animal form. The transformation is extremely difficult and dangerous, requiring years of practice and registration with the Ministry of Magic. Unregistered Animagi include James Potter (a stag), Sirius Black (a large black dog), Peter Pettigrew (a rat), and Rita Skeeter (a beetle). Professor McGonagall is a registered Animagus who transforms into a tabby cat with square markings around her eyes matching her spectacles.

Occlumency and Legilimency are the magical arts of defending and penetrating the mind, respectively. Occlumency allows a practitioner to shield their thoughts and memories from external penetration. Legilimency is the ability to extract emotions and memories from another person's mind. Severus Snape was a master of both arts, and Lord Voldemort was considered the most accomplished Legilimens in history. Harry Potter shared a unique mental connection with Voldemort through his scar, which was in fact a fragment of Voldemort's soul — an accidental Horcrux.

Horcruxes represent the darkest and most terrible form of magic. A Horcrux is an object in which a dark wizard has concealed a fragment of their soul, achieved by committing murder — the supreme act of evil — which fractures the soul, allowing a piece to be sealed in an external object. As long as a Horcrux exists, the creator cannot truly die. Lord Voldemort created six Horcruxes intentionally (his diary, Marvolo Gaunt's ring, Slytherin's locket, Hufflepuff's cup, Ravenclaw's diadem, and his snake Nagini) and one accidentally (Harry Potter himself). The destruction of all seven soul fragments was necessary before Voldemort could be finally defeated.
