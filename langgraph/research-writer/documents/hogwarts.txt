Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry is the premier magical educational institution in Britain, founded over a thousand years ago by four of the greatest witches and wizards of the age: Godric Gryffindor, Helga Hufflepuff, Rowena Ravenclaw, and Salazar Slytherin. The castle is located in the Scottish Highlands and is hidden from Muggles by powerful enchantments — to non-magical people, it appears as nothing more than a dangerous ruin with a sign reading "Keep Out."

Students are admitted at age eleven and receive their acceptance letter delivered by owl post. The school year runs from September 1st to late June. Students travel to Hogwarts aboard the Hogwarts Express, a scarlet steam engine that departs from Platform Nine and Three-Quarters at King's Cross Station in London. To reach the platform, students must walk through the apparently solid barrier between platforms nine and ten.

Upon arrival, first-year students are sorted into one of four houses by the Sorting Hat, an ancient enchanted hat that once belonged to Godric Gryffindor. The hat reads the mind of each student and places them in the house that best matches their qualities. Gryffindor values bravery, daring, and chivalry. Ravenclaw prizes wit, learning, and wisdom. Hufflepuff values hard work, patience, loyalty, and fair play. Slytherin values ambition, cunning, leadership, and resourcefulness. Students live in their house dormitories for their entire time at Hogwarts, and houses compete throughout the year for the House Cup by earning and losing points.

The castle itself is enormous and labyrinthine, with 142 staircases — some of which move, vanish, or lead somewhere different on Fridays. There are also doors that aren't really doors, walls pretending to be doors, and secret passages hidden behind tapestries and suits of armour. The castle contains hundreds of portraits whose subjects can move and talk, and ghosts who roam the corridors freely. Each house has its own ghost: Nearly Headless Nick for Gryffindor, the Grey Lady for Ravenclaw, the Fat Friar for Hufflepuff, and the Bloody Baron for Slytherin.

Key locations within the castle include the Great Hall, where students eat their meals at long house tables beneath an enchanted ceiling that mirrors the sky outside. The headmaster's office is accessible via a moving spiral staircase hidden behind a stone gargoyle that requires a password (usually the name of a sweet). The Room of Requirement is a secret room on the seventh floor that only appears when a person has real need of it, transforming into whatever the seeker requires.

The curriculum at Hogwarts covers a wide range of magical subjects. Core subjects include Transfiguration (taught by Professor McGonagall, involving changing the form or appearance of an object), Charms (taught by the diminutive Professor Flitwick), Potions (taught in the dungeons, first by Severus Snape and later by Horace Slughorn), Defence Against the Dark Arts (which had a different teacher almost every year due to a rumoured curse on the position), Herbology (taught by Professor Sprout in the greenhouses), History of Magic (taught by the ghost Professor Binns), and Astronomy. Elective subjects available from third year include Care of Magical Creatures, Divination, Arithmancy, Ancient Runes, and Muggle Studies.

Hogwarts is governed by a headmaster or headmistress. Albus Dumbledore served as headmaster for decades and was widely regarded as the greatest wizard of his time. The school is also home to the caretaker Argus Filch (a Squib who resents the students) and his cat Mrs. Norris, the groundskeeper Rubeus Hagrid (a half-giant with a love for dangerous creatures), and the house-elves who work in the kitchens preparing the feasts that appear magically on the tables in the Great Hall.

The Hogwarts grounds include the Black Lake (home to a giant squid and a colony of merpeople), the Forbidden Forest (inhabited by centaurs, unicorns, Acromantulas, thestrals, and other creatures), Hagrid's hut on the edge of the forest, the Quidditch pitch, and several greenhouses. The Whomping Willow, a violent magical tree planted to conceal a secret passage to the Shrieking Shack in Hogsmeade, guards one of the entrances to the grounds.
